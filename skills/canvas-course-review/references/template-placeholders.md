# Master/blueprint placeholders

Apply this exception to courses identified by the user as master/blueprint templates or confirmed by source evidence to serve that purpose. API blueprint status alone may not identify every institutional master. Record course_type=master_blueprint and describe the template scope. An unpublished course/page alone does not prove instructor-completion intent.

Inspect the scaffold, facilitator instructions, or authored completion note. When these establish an intentional preparation item, rate the relevant provision Present with verified evidence, deferred=false, and a reminder naming the instructor task, location and completion timing (usually before learners enter). State that Present recognizes template design; do not imply the biography, meeting link or publication is already finished. Intentionally unpublished preparation pages are included in the review, not discarded as unused.

## Possible criteria

These are candidates, not automatic exemptions. Apply only to the instructor-owned portion actually supported by a scaffold or documented plan; assess the remaining requirements normally.

| CU | Possible instructor-completion item | Reminder example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Instructor contact information, office hours, instructor-specific interaction guidelines | Replace contact/office-hour placeholders before opening the course; retain institutional support and grievance policies. |
| 3 | Instructor-specific course expectations or technical setup details | Personalize the indicated expectations/setup fields and confirm consistency with the established course requirements. |
| 6 | Planned navigation/tools awaiting instructor setup | Configure the identified meeting/tool destination and confirm navigation before opening; unexplained unused tools still need clarification. |
| 12 | Instructor-owned instructions with clear completion prompts | Complete and publish the specified directions; missing core assignment instructions are still a design concern. |
| 24 | Welcome/resource or instructor-contact elements within an otherwise complete resource/interaction design | Personalize the identified resource; retain the designed resource variety and biblical integration. |
| 28 | Announcement schedule, planned substantive contact, office hours and feedback timing | Finalize instructor schedule and contact details in the provided interaction plan. |
| 29 | Instructor-specific communication channels, response expectations and modeled interaction | Personalize and publish the communication template; retain learner-to-learner expectations and examples. |
| 30 | Instructor biography, photo, welcome message/video or introduction page | Add the instructor introduction and publish the welcome page before learners enter. |
| 31 | Office-hour/meeting links or welcome/community activity setup | Set up the planned meeting and introduction activity; retain the designed opportunities for community. |
| 34 | Instructor-selectable policy fields in an established grading-policy template | Complete permitted policy choices and check consistency with the institutional grading and late-work rules. |
| 38 | Instructor contact/setup fields for a documented timed-assessment accommodation process | Complete the contact/setup information before timed assessments; required time-limit notice and preparation guidance still need evidence. |
| 39 | A designed course-feedback activity awaiting instructor scheduling/publication | Schedule and publish the provided feedback activity; an undocumented future survey is not sufficient. |

CU 1 and 30 are the most direct cases. The other criteria qualify only when the actual scaffold or plan addresses their design intent. A bare page title, blank page, generic TBD, or promise of future material without enough context cannot establish an entire criterion. Ask for clarification or retain a null rating when intent is unresolved; use a revision for a demonstrated non-placeholder deficiency.

## Boundaries and reporting

- No blanket Present rating for every criterion in a master course. Objectives/alignment, assessment criteria, core readings/activities, Christian integration, and required institutional policies remain substantive design checks.
- Inspect accessibility of existing template content. Missing caption evidence, inaccessible documents, or unknown tool compliance remain deficiencies or verification gaps; future personalization does not prove accessibility.
- Preserve configured publication states. A planned unpublished instructor page is Present as a template provision, not verified as learner-visible. Accidental unpublished core content or contradictory availability needs separate assessment.
- Keep preparation reminders separate from the revision checklist. If another part of the same criterion needs revision, retain that rating and its narrative coverage, alongside the instructor reminder.
- In actual offerings, assess whether required pre-launch personalization has been completed under the requested scope. Do not judge subsequent teaching execution or learner outcomes.
- Apply this same policy in level-3 independent confirmation. The reviewer must see raw template instructions, not merely the primary reviewer’s assertion that a placeholder is intentional.
