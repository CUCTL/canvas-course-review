# Review policy

Version 1.12, October 27, 2026. Internal reviewer policy; not an amendment to official university guidance. CUOCDR v0.9 is the rating authority. The preserved rubric has 42 items numbered 1–24 and 26–43; do not add item 25. Preserve category order and criterion wording.

Use [internal review checks](internal-review-checks.md) to compare assessment instructions across locations and apply the designated CTL accessibility checklist with bounded verification. Master/blueprint and optional-training interpretations are internal operating rules; do not describe them as official university exceptions.

## Ratings and evidence

Ratings: Present, Minor Revision, Moderate Revision, Major Revision, N/A. A null rating represents pending verification, not a sixth rating. Every rated criterion needs concise evidence and resolvable source IDs. Revision items need a concrete action plan; Present needs observed support; N/A needs a concise, course-specific `na_reason` explaining why the criterion does not apply, supported by the observed design. Show this comment in the Action/verification column and any Kuali Action Plan field. Missing evidence or an unfinished check does not justify N/A. Unresolved criteria need a verification step and uncertainty.

Evidence states:
- verified: inspected evidence supports the conclusion within the stated scope.
- not_evidenced: the required element was not found after inspecting relevant locations; document the search scope. This can support a revision when the search establishes an actual absence.
- not_verified: access, sampling, missing policy, or technical limits prevent a conclusion; leave rating null.
- not_applicable: a legitimate N/A, with rationale.

For a confirmed master/blueprint template, treat intentional instructor-completion placeholders and intentionally unpublished preparation pages as **Present**, with verified evidence of the template scaffold, deferred=false, and a concrete instructor completion reminder. Present here means the template design includes the intended provision, not that instructor-specific content or publication is already complete. Record manifest.course_type=master_blueprint and criterion.template_placeholder with scope and reminder; see [template placeholder guidance](template-placeholders.md). Do not automatically pass an entire criterion if unrelated requirements are missing, infer intent from unpublished status alone, or excuse missing core content/accessibility. In an actual course offering, review readiness against its stated scope; the template exception does not carry over automatically. The legacy deferred flag remains readable for historical reports; do not use it for intentional template placeholders in new reviews.

Source time bands are guidance: Minor <20 minutes, Moderate <40 minutes, Major 60+ minutes. They overlap and leave 40–59 minutes unclear. Do not invent a numeric classification rule; explain ambiguous choices for instructor judgment.

## Presentation

Write narrative feedback and action plans for general educators without instructional design training. Technical evidence and the preserved rubric may retain specialist wording. Use one independent [writing style agent](writing-style-review.md) at every level; it is distinct from blind course assessment. Keep its assessment internal and preserve factual meaning when applying edits. Follow [the narrative guide](narrative-feedback-guidelines.md) to explain supported strengths, the main patterns, their implications for students and where to begin; keep complete repair instructions in the tables. This purpose is part of the writing agent's approval.

Text rating labels are authoritative; do not rely on color/icons. Review tables contain ID, criterion, rating, evidence, and action/verification. Show scope, snapshot, rubric version, source references, coverage limitations, deferred fields, priority actions, and mechanical grammar separately. Keep internal policy rules, exceptions, audit history and verification provenance out of the narrative; mention only limitations that materially affect a finding or next step. User verification/corrections can support findings when their source is recorded internally. Routine preparation reminders stay in detailed report sections unless tied to a confirmed design finding. Write the narrative after findings are finalized, then display it immediately after the course identity/date/mode/status and a separate report-level limitations note only when material, before the rubric tables. Put full scope/limitations and stage outcomes after the tables and grammar findings. Keep the detailed source register, mappings, file checklist and inventory in a separate evidence register linked from the main report; suggestions must not become new unsupported deficiencies.

When related findings need one coordinated correction, follow [the internal grouping guidance](internal-review-checks.md#2-group-related-findings-into-a-coordinated-repair). Preserve separate criterion ratings and evidence, explain the shared concern and next step once in the overview, and retain the specific task/location pairs in each affected table cell. Do not count repeated descriptions as separate repairs.

Report-generation status is separate from course quality. completed means all stages required by the selected review level were performed, even if technical/policy verification remains pending and is explicitly disclosed. partial means scope or a report stage remains unfinished. failed means no usable review was produced. Never turn failure text or missing stages into a clean success.

## Independent confirmation

For level 3, before presenting a new review, obtain a separate agent’s blind assessment of the same saved raw evidence and controlling policy. Freeze both assessments before comparison; reconcile against evidence rather than averaging. Then audit the final report and narrative for consistency, documenting that comparison is no longer blind. Reuse existing browser evidence unless a concrete gap requires more inspection. Preserve assessment/audit artifacts and disclose incomplete or contaminated confirmation. See [the confirmation workflow](blind-confirmation.md).

## Review levels

Offer three levels when the user has not selected one: API content + narrative (1), API content + necessary browser verification + narrative (2; recommended), and API content + necessary browser verification + blind assessment + narrative (3). Wait for an explicit user selection before course collection or review; silence and a preselected option do not select a level. The rubric, design-only boundary, grammar pass, narrative checklist, and report format apply to all levels. Level 1 cannot establish browser-dependent behavior; leave those conclusions unverified, with explicit limits and next steps. Levels 1–2 do not require independent confirmation. All levels require a primary consistency check and validation. Level 3 requires blind assessment, reconciliation, and the final policy audit. See [review levels](review-levels.md). Do not infer a deficiency from a check intentionally outside the selected level, or silently downgrade unfinished level-3 confirmation.

## Design-only boundary

Review planned and authored course design, not course delivery. Interaction criteria concern documented expectations, planned substantive contact, communication guidance, and opportunities designed into the course. Feedback criteria concern stated policies, assessment criteria, self-review opportunities, and designed feedback mechanisms. Do not evaluate actual instructor responsiveness, feedback execution/quality, student engagement/achievement, attendance, or actual accommodation implementation. Do not turn empty activity histories, unavailable real-learner sessions, or absent posted grades into design deficiencies.

Configured dates, navigation/visibility, media playback, document accessibility, quiz instructions and preview controls may be checked as design evidence. Actual enrolled-learner testing and delivery outcomes are outside this skill; their absence is a scope boundary, not unfinished design review. Missing documented design intent can remain pending, with a request to clarify the plan. In master/blueprint templates, treat intentional instructor personalization scaffolds as Present with completion reminders, as described above. Include at least three practical next-term teaching tips in the narrative as unscored suggestions grounded in the reviewed design. They do not establish delivery deficiencies or change ratings. Follow [the narrative guide](narrative-feedback-guidelines.md) for their wording, placement and word limit.
